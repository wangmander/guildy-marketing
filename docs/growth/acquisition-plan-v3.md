# Guildy Acquisition Plan v3 (locked)

Four critique rounds in. Stop debating, execute. This is the operating plan.

## The model

Owned pages capture intent. Research earns attention. External voices establish credibility. Shareable prep creates distribution. The product converts and retains. Not owned vs earned. All of it, in this order.

## What we optimize

Not raw eyeballs. Paying users, found where job seekers already are, biased toward the high-stakes moments where the $19.99 lives: active final-round loops, onsites next week, layoff communities interviewing now. Free Quick Prep hooks them; Deep Prep converts the ones in a real loop. Top-of-funnel "thinking about a job change" browsing is low willingness to pay. Chase pain.

## The metric stack

| Layer | Metric |
|---|---|
| Reach | qualified landing sessions |
| Acquisition | cost per first prep generated |
| Activation | % of visitors who generate prep |
| Retention | second meaningful action within 14 days (retained preparer) |
| Revenue | free to Deep conversion, by acquisition source |
| AEO | AI-referred preps (utm_source=chatgpt.com etc.), not raw citations |

prep_generated is the channel and activation event. Retained preparer is the business signal. Mass acquisition into a leaky funnel just drains.

## The two engines v2 was missing

1. Free-tool growth loop. The only mass mechanism you have. The free prep becomes a shareable object, not a static landing surface.
2. Conversion spine. So mass does not leak. prep then save then prep your next round then upgrade. Partly in flight via the post-signup fixes. Not retention gamification (still tabled), just the product working end to end.

## Sequence (order matters more than the percentages)

Phase 0. Measurement + stop the leak. Full funnel in PostHog (largely wired), add retained-preparer. Anonymous prep flawless. Save + next-round transition. Results safely shareable + referral attribution.

Phase 1. The growth loop. Shareable branded prep result link, sensitive fields hidden by default. "Generate prep for this role" on every shared result. Job-specific deep link that preloads a public JD, so a shared link is a working demo for that exact role. Referral attribution. Embeddable example for coaches and newsletters. NOT a Chrome extension yet; prove links first.

Phase 2. One defensible research asset, from PUBLIC job listings only, never customer resumes or pasted JDs. Claim only what the data supports: requirement frequency, AI-experience demand, startup vs enterprise, senior vs staff shifts. Publish methodology and limits. Do not claim "what hiring managers probe," which is unmeasurable. Fan the one asset into a page, a downloadable report, 10 charts for social, creator pitches, newsletter pitches, Reddit material, press, and page content. One campaign, not 30 cold messages.

Phase 3. Three high-intent owned pages first, not eight: interview questions from a JD, recruiter screen prep, hiring manager prep. Each with the live generator above the fold. Island Test: question H1, then a 40 to 60 word direct answer. FAQ + SoftwareApplication schema. Expand only if these convert.

Phase 4. Fire the ammunition. The gift is the pitch: send each creator, coach, bootcamp, and editorial contact a finished Guildy prep for a real role their audience cares about. Reddit, Blind, Discord, layoff and bootcamp communities, manual, never automated, seeded with the research findings. GitHub awesome-list PRs.

Phase 5. Small paid validation. Google exact-match + Reddit ads, separate landing per message, judged on cost per prep, cost per retained preparer, and paid conversion by source.

Phase 6. Product Hunt burst (after the funnel is strong and the post-signup fixes are live) + directories (one-day housekeeping, free ones only, then stop).

## Where job seekers actually are, and how we meet them

| Where | The move |
|---|---|
| At the JD on LinkedIn/Indeed | job-specific deep-link prep; later the extension |
| In communities (Reddit, Blind, Discord, Slack, bootcamp cohorts) | manual high-value presence + shareable links spread here + research as discussion fuel |
| Searching ("interview questions for X") | owned high-intent pages |
| Asking AI ("best interview prep tool") | citation from research + Reddit consensus + owned pages as the primary source |
| Following creators and coaches | borrowed-audience outreach, gift-as-pitch |

## Stat honesty (so we do not get re-seduced)

The "94% earned media," "40% Reddit," "239% lift," "72% get zero citations" numbers thrown around this week are descriptive, correlational, vendor-shaped, volatile, or misattributed. Act on mechanisms, not stat decks. The mechanisms that are actually true: a 0-follower brand needs off-domain presence; owned pages do three jobs nothing else does (capture intent, be the primary source, convert); proprietary research is the best self-producible citation magnet; the product as a shareable object is the only thing that scales without linear effort.
